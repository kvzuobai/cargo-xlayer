How Cargo worksCargo 如何运作

A ship on X Layer, built on TapeOut. Circuits load onto berths and earn CARGO every voyage. The OKB paid is split by fixed rules into locked liquidity, a reserve, buy and burn, and the team.

基于 TapeOut、运行在 X Layer 上的一艘货轮。电路装上泊位，每个航次获得 CARGO。用户支付的 OKB 按固定规则分配到锁定流动性、储备、回购销毁和团队。

Version 1 · 23 Oct 2026 · Season 1 · All numbers are contract constants on X Layer mainnet.版本 1 · 2026 年 10 月 23 日 · 第 1 季 · 所有数字均为 X Layer 主网合约常量。

00Summary概要

TapeOut lets anyone mint transistors and tape out circuits on X Layer. Cargo gives those circuits a use.

  • For users: load a circuit onto a berth and earn CARGO every 5 minutes, plus OKB from pool trading fees. Unload any time.
  • For TapeOut and BEM: every mint, load and charter adds OKB to locked BEM-OKB liquidity and brings new tapeouts.
  • For other projects: charter a berth to send your token to users who have put OKB into circuits.

The contracts are not upgradable. Splits, emission and fees are constants. There is no premine, no team allocation of CARGO, no price promise, and no chance-based element.

TapeOut 让任何人都能在 X Layer 上铸造晶体管、流片成电路。Cargo 让这些电路有用处。

  • 对用户：把电路装上泊位，每 5 分钟获得 CARGO，并获得池子交易手续费分成的 OKB。随时可以卸货。
  • 对 TapeOut 和 BEM：每一次铸造、装货、包租都会把 OKB 加入锁定的 BEM-OKB 流动性，并带来新的流片量。
  • 对其他项目：包租泊位，把你的代币发给真正把 OKB 投入电路的用户。

合约不可升级。分配比例、排放和费用都是常量。没有预挖，团队没有 CARGO 份额，不承诺价格，没有任何随机机制。

01Mechanics机制

Three steps

  1. Mint NAND on the Cargo season processor. Season 1 price: 0.002 OKB per NAND, cap 2,000,000 NAND.
  2. Tape out a circuit on TapeOut (TapeOut charges its own tapeout fee).
  3. Load the circuit onto one of 25 berths, paying a dock fee of 5% of circuit value.

Power

Power = transistors in the circuit × the season transistor price. Example: 120 NAND × 0.002 OKB = 0.24 OKB power, dock fee 0.012 OKB. Season 1 cap: 1 OKB of power per wallet.

Voyages and berths

  • A voyage is 5 minutes (288 per day). Emission is split into 29 equal shares: 25 berths + 4 for Express.
  • Each berth gets 1/29 of emission, about 0.599 CARGO per voyage, shared by power among the circuits on it. Rewards accrue continuously.
  • An empty berth pays nothing, so a quiet berth pays more. A circuit can move to another berth once per voyage.
  • Unload returns the circuit to your wallet. The dock fee and the transistors used are not refunded.

Official TapeOut circuits

CPUs approved by the team (48h wait, max 20) can load only on berths B20–B25, count at 50% power and do not receive OKB from pool fees. This opens Cargo to the wider TapeOut ecosystem while limiting it to at most 6 of 29 shares.

三步上船

  1. 在 Cargo 季度处理器上铸造 NAND。第 1 季价格：每个 NAND 0.002 OKB，上限 2,000,000 个。
  2. 在 TapeOut 上流片成电路（TapeOut 收取其流片费）。
  3. 把电路装到 25 个泊位之一，支付电路价值 5% 的码头费。

算力

算力 = 电路中的晶体管数量 × 本季晶体管价格。例：120 NAND × 0.002 OKB = 0.24 OKB 算力，码头费 0.012 OKB。第 1 季上限：每个钱包 1 OKB 算力。

航次与泊位

  • 每个航次 5 分钟（每天 288 个）。排放平分为 29 份：25 个泊位 + 4 份快线。
  • 每个泊位获得 1/29 的排放，约每航次 0.599 CARGO，由该泊位上的电路按算力分配。奖励连续累积。
  • 空泊位不发放，所以人少的泊位份额更大。每个航次电路最多可移动一次泊位。
  • 卸货后电路回到你的钱包。码头费和已使用的晶体管不退。

TapeOut 官方电路

团队批准的 CPU（等待 48 小时，最多 20 个）只能装在 B20–B25 泊位，按 50% 算力计，不获得池子手续费的 OKB。这让更广的 TapeOut 生态可以参与，同时最多只占 29 份中的 6 份。

CARGO is only earned by loading circuits. Emission for empty berths is never minted, so real supply stays below the schedule. The token has no owner powers: no pause, no blacklist, no extra mint.

Uses: buy Express seats (burned), redeem a pro-rata share of the reserve, trade in the locked CARGO-OKB pool. Burn paths: Express seats, buy and burn from fees, Season 2+ loading.

CARGO 只能通过装货获得。空泊位对应的排放永远不会铸造，所以实际供应量低于计划。代币没有所有者权限：不能暂停、不能拉黑、不能增发。

用途：购买快线座位（销毁）、按比例赎回储备、在锁定的 CARGO-OKB 池交易。销毁途径：快线座位、手续费回购销毁、第 2 季起装货销毁。

03Express快线

  • 4 of 29 shares go to hourly Express blocks: about 28.7 CARGO per block.
  • 20 seats per block. Seats are sold only for the next block. Max 2 seats per wallet and 1 per circuit.
  • Seats are paid in CARGO, and that CARGO is burned. Price starts at 10 CARGO; ×1.1 if the last block sold 18+ seats, ×0.9 if under 10; minimum 1 CARGO.
  • The block reward is shared by power among seat holders, captured when the seat is bought. Claim after the block ends.
  • 29 份中的 4 份分给每小时的快线时段：每时段约 28.7 CARGO。
  • 每时段 20 个座位，只出售下一时段的座位。每个钱包最多 2 个，每个电路 1 个。
  • 座位用 CARGO 支付，这些 CARGO 会被销毁。起始价 10 CARGO；上一时段售出 ≥18 个则 ×1.1，少于 10 个则 ×0.9；最低 1 CARGO。
  • 时段奖励按购买座位时的算力分给座位持有者。时段结束后领取。

04Charter包租

  • A project rents one berth for 7–90 days at 0.0005 OKB per voyage, paid up front and not refundable. 7 days = 2,016 voyages = 1.008 OKB.
  • The project adds its own ERC-20 token. It streams evenly every voyage to the circuits on that berth, on top of CARGO.
  • Token emitted while the berth is empty goes back to the project after the charter ends (Reclaim).
  • At most 10 of 25 berths can be chartered at once. Fee-on-transfer and rebasing tokens are rejected.

For the project, it is a measurable way to reach users who have already put OKB into circuits.

  • 项目方租用一个泊位 7–90 天，每航次 0.0005 OKB，预付且不退。7 天 = 2,016 个航次 = 1.008 OKB。
  • 项目方放入自己的 ERC-20 代币，每个航次平均发放给该泊位上的电路，在 CARGO 之外额外获得。
  • 泊位空置期间发放的代币，包租结束后退回项目方（Reclaim）。
  • 同时最多 10/25 个泊位可被包租。转账收费型和弹性供应型代币会被拒绝。

对项目方来说，这是触达真正把 OKB 投入电路的用户的一种可衡量方式。

05Bills of lading提单

  • Each loaded circuit is a bill of lading NFT (ERC-721 "CBOL") owned by the loader.
  • Bills move only through the built-in market (list / buy). Fee: 1% of the higher of price and power, to voyage fees.
  • Pending rewards are paid to the seller at the sale. The buyer keeps the same berth. The Season 1 wallet cap applies to the buyer.
  • 每个装船电路是一张提单 NFT（ERC-721「CBOL」），归装货人所有。
  • 提单只能通过内置市场转让（挂单 / 购买）。费用：价格与算力中较高者的 1%，计入航运费。
  • 待领奖励在成交时付给卖方。买方保留原泊位。第 1 季钱包上限对买方同样适用。

07Liquidity & BEM流动性与 BEM

  • Protocol-owned liquidity: two full-range Uniswap v3 positions (CARGO-OKB, BEM-OKB). The position NFTs are held by contracts with no remove function and no owner.
  • BEM-OKB receives 35% of mint OKB in Season 1 (rising each season), 45% of dock fees and 35% of voyage fees. BEM earned as trading fees is added back into the pool.
  • CARGO-OKB pool fees: 70% of the OKB buys and burns CARGO, 30% streams to circuits on board over 24 hours. CARGO earned as fees is burned.
  • Swaps check the 15-minute TWAP: spot must be within ~2% and the minimum output is TWAP −3%, to limit sandwiching.
  • At launch the CARGO pool is an OKB-only bid at 0.00005 OKB per CARGO until holders sell into it.

Every use of Cargo adds to BEM-OKB liquidity that cannot be withdrawn. Cargo reports on-chain amounts only and makes no claim about the BEM price.

  • 协议自有流动性：两个全区间 Uniswap v3 仓位（CARGO-OKB、BEM-OKB）。仓位 NFT 由合约持有，没有撤出函数，也没有所有者。
  • BEM-OKB 获得第 1 季铸造 OKB 的 35%（每季增加）、码头费的 45%、航运费的 35%。作为手续费获得的 BEM 会再加回池子。
  • CARGO-OKB 池手续费：OKB 的 70% 回购销毁 CARGO，30% 在 24 小时内付给在船电路。作为手续费获得的 CARGO 会被销毁。
  • 兑换会检查 15 分钟 TWAP：现价偏离需在约 2% 以内，最小成交量为 TWAP −3%，以限制夹子攻击。
  • 上线初期，CARGO 池只有 0.00005 OKB/CARGO 的 OKB 买单，直到持有者卖入。

每一次使用 Cargo 都会加深无法撤出的 BEM-OKB 流动性。Cargo 只报告链上数额，不对 BEM 价格作任何表述。

08Reserve储备

The reserve receives 10% of mint OKB and 15% of voyage fees.

Reserve share = free OKB in the reserve ÷ (CARGO supply + unclaimed CARGO)

  • Any holder can redeem: CARGO is burned at request, 1% stays in the reserve, OKB is paid after 24 hours.
  • The share changes as supply grows and falls when emission outpaces inflows. It is not a fixed floor and not a price promise.

储备获得铸造 OKB 的 10% 和航运费的 15%。

储备份额 = 储备中可用 OKB ÷（CARGO 供应量 + 未领取 CARGO）

  • 任何持有者都可赎回：申请时销毁 CARGO，1% 留在储备，24 小时后支付 OKB。
  • 份额随供应量变化，排放快于流入时会下降。它不是固定底价，也不是价格承诺。

09Seasons赛季

  • Each season is a new TapeOut processor created by the Cargo Dock, at least 30 days after the last one, with a transistor price at least ×1.25 the last.
  • From Season 2, loading a circuit also burns CARGO worth 20% of its value (priced by the pool TWAP oracle).
  • Season 1 circuits keep sailing in later seasons.
  • 每一季是 Cargo Dock 创建的新 TapeOut 处理器，距上一季至少 30 天，晶体管价格至少为上一季的 ×1.25。
  • 从第 2 季起，装货时还会销毁价值为电路 20% 的 CARGO（按池子 TWAP 预言机计价）。
  • 第 1 季的电路在之后的赛季继续航行。

10KeeperKeeper

Routine calls move OKB along the fixed splits: dock.sweep, miner.sweepVoyageFees, bemPol.add, cargoPol.add / addBid, harvest, burner.burn. A keeper bot runs them every 15 minutes. All are permissionless: if the bot stops, anyone can run them from Dock → Advanced. None of them can send funds to a private wallet.

例行调用按固定比例转移 OKB：dock.sweep、miner.sweepVoyageFees、bemPol.add、cargoPol.add / addBid、harvest、burner.burn。Keeper 机器人每 15 分钟执行一次。所有调用都无需权限：机器人停止时，任何人都可以在「码头」→「高级」中执行。这些调用都不能把资金转到私人钱包。

12Risks风险

  • Experimental and unaudited. Unit tests, fork tests and a Slither scan; no outside audit.
  • TapeOut dependency. TapeOut circuit and processor contracts are upgradable by the TapeOut team.
  • Market risk. CARGO may trade at any price or have little liquidity. The reserve share falls as supply grows.
  • No refunds. Dock fees, transistors used, charter fees and Express seats are not refunded.
  • Gaming. Coordinated wallets can affect berth crowding and Express seat prices. Caps limit this but do not remove it.
  • Owner key. A compromised owner key could queue a fee-route change; it would show on the site 48h before taking effect.

Use only what you can lose.

  • 实验性，未经审计。有单元测试、分叉测试和 Slither 扫描；没有外部审计。
  • 依赖 TapeOut。TapeOut 电路和处理器合约可由 TapeOut 团队升级。
  • 市场风险。CARGO 可能以任何价格交易，流动性也可能很低。储备份额随供应量增加而下降。
  • 不退款。码头费、已使用的晶体管、包租费和快线座位均不退。
  • 策略操纵。协同的钱包可能影响泊位拥挤度和快线座位价格。上限能减少但不能消除这种情况。
  • 所有者私钥。私钥泄露可能导致费用去向变更被排队；它会在生效前 48 小时显示在网站上。

只投入你能承受损失的金额。
